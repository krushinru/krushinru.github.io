> This is an archive page for the first version of HabitHit. The current version 2.0 lives on the [main project page](/en/projects/habithit/).

## About

HabitHit 1.0 was a minimal Telegram Mini App for tracking habits, launched in 2025. The first version bet everything on privacy: all habit data was stored only in the user's personal Telegram CloudStorage, never sent to third-party servers.

The service solved a simple motivation problem: people often give up because they cannot see progress. HabitHit made achievements visible, which helped users keep going.

### Key features

**Visual tracker**

Progress was displayed clearly, with weekly, monthly and yearly charts. This helped users see their momentum and understand how consistently they were working toward their goals.

**Data privacy**

All habit data was stored in the user's personal Telegram CloudStorage. Only the account owner had access — no data was sent to third-party servers.

**Cross-platform access**

Because the app ran inside Telegram, habits stayed in sync across a user's devices without extra setup. Mark a habit as done on your phone, and the update was immediately visible on desktop.

## Why we restarted the project

The first version proved demand but hit its limits: without a server there was no shared progress, no friends, no leagues and no cross-device sync. That led to a full rebuild — [HabitHit 2.0](/en/projects/habithit/).
