## About

HabitHit is a habit tracker that turns everyday routine into a short ritual: three to five missions a day, one tap each, and a visible reward. No long to-do lists, no guilt.

The first version lived inside Telegram: native design, no own servers, all data stored within the messenger itself. But it failed at the core job — it didn't make users want to stay inside a habit.

Version 2.0 changed the tone and put the emphasis on gamification and social features — following the example of Duolingo and Chess.com — and moved beyond Telegram: PWA and full mobile apps.

**60+ templates across 15 categories.** From water and sleep to creativity and digital balance. No need to phrase a habit from scratch — pick a ready template, set the days, start checking off. You can also create a habit with any parameters you like.

**AI coach.** The assistant analyzes your completion patterns and suggests when to move a reminder or simplify a goal.

**Friends and leagues.** Find friends by nickname, see their progress in weekly leagues and compete in rankings.

**Every platform.** HabitHit is available as a web version, a Telegram Mini App and an Android app. An iOS version is coming soon. Progress can be synced across platforms.

## How it works

1. Onboarding picks a starter set of three habits for your goal — more energy, calm, focus, or a healthy rhythm.
2. Each day you open the app and check off completed missions — one tap each.
3. Closing the day's plan earns bonus XP.
4. XP levels you up and grows your world: at level seven the plot becomes a big house, at level ten — a thriving town.
5. The AI coach notices patterns and suggests micro-adjustments so you don't fall off track.
